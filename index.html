04

Repair status bot

Auto repair, appliance and device repair shops

Half the calls are "is my car ready yet?" And when a technician finds a worn part, the car sits on the lift until someone gets the owner on the phone to approve it.

A Telegram bot that keeps customers posted as the car moves through the shop, and sends extra work for approval with one tap: approve, decline or call me first. The service desk runs it from their own phone. Nothing goes out after 9 pm; updates made at night are reworded and sent in the morning.

05

Crew dispatch board

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning: any business with crews

The day is planned on a whiteboard. Two jobs land at the same time, a tech gets ten minutes to cross town, and a customer told "morning" sees someone at 1 pm.

A Windows app for whoever plans the day. Drag jobs onto technicians and it checks everything as you go: overlaps, drive time between stops, the customer's arrival window, shifts, who does which trade. Auto-schedule fills the gaps, then it prints a route sheet for each tech.
